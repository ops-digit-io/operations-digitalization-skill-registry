# Handover to Internal Communications

**Status.** Internal Communications is a department outside OD. A staff census in the knowledge base records the role as central, in HR and Communications, with the task of scalable communication around activities, keeping the image, sorting the funnel and managing expectations (`Document Texts/Department Documents/Digital or IT Staff in Business.md`, row Internal Communications; a person-level file, so use the role only). OD's own January 2026 marketing-template initiative planned to define the process, responsibilities and channels but left its results slide empty (`department-formats.md`). No note in the knowledge base was found that describes IC's process (a keyword search of `department knowledge base` for internal communication, newsletter and intranet gave 17 hits in 13 files, mostly plant and deck notes; searched 2026-11-10, hits not opened, so a relevant note may exist; the archive was not searched). The owner will supply the Internal Communications brief. Until then everything marked ⟨to confirm⟩ is open, and this file must not be filled from general knowledge.

## Who approves what

| Approval | Who | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The story is true and the plant may be named | Plant owner (role) | Written |
| OD tells it | OD lead | Proposed; not in the department decision rights |
| Channel, form, date, final wording, publication | Internal Communications | IC decides; OD does not publish |
