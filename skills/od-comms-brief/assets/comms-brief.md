# Brief: ⟨use case⟩ at ⟨plant⟩

## Objective

⟨One line: what the audience should know or do afterwards, and how OD would see it (a request, a question, a use case running elsewhere). No target figures.⟩

## Message

⟨One sentence, present tense: what changed, where, for whom.⟩

## Audience

- Other plants and key users: ⟨variant, one or two sentences⟩
- BA Operations leadership: ⟨variant⟩
- All employees: ⟨variant⟩
- OPEX and PPI/ME community: ⟨variant⟩
- Line and shift supervisors (only where the change touches how a line is run; Internal Communications confirms): ⟨what changes for me, what do I do⟩

## Proof points

| Claim | Evidence class | Source | Worded as |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⟨claim⟩ | ⟨CALCULATION / ESTIMATION / not yet assessed⟩ | ⟨file or register entry, date⟩ | ⟨result / claim⟩ |

## Call to action

⟨What the reader does next and where: for example, a plant requests the use case through the catalogue via ⟨route⟩. Contact as a role.⟩

## Channels and timing

⟨As Internal Communications states them in its intake. Open until the Internal Communications brief is supplied.⟩

## Sensitivity screen

⟨Confirm: no individuals named, no host names or addresses, no links to internal systems, no supplier or customer detail, nothing the plant owner marked as not for release.⟩

## People content

⟨State "none" (roles and screenshots only), or list each photo, video or quote of a member of staff with the consent or the documented balancing of interests behind it.⟩
