## Pre-send check
- [ ] Budget has a value or `<...>` with an owner
- [ ] Timing has start, end, first version, three milestones
- [ ] Out of scope is written
- [ ] Sponsor, lead and team named or flagged
- [ ] Every high risk has a treatment that is in plan and budget
- [ ] Each sub-target has at least three work packages (and no more than twelve sub-targets)
- [ ] Reused projects are cited
